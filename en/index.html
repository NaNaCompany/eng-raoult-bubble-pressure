FORMULA

P = Σ(xᵢpᵢ*); Σxᵢ = 1

Assumptions

Required: all vapor pressures evaluated at the same temperature, ideal liquid and vapor phases, and vapor-liquid equilibrium. Excluded: activity-coefficient corrections, fugacity corrections and chemical reaction. A nonvolatile component may use zero vapor pressure. Liquid mole fractions must sum to 1; rounding differences up to 1e-10 are accepted and normalized. Enter liquid composition, rather than vapor composition.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
